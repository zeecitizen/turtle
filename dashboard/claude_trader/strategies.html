S1 — UHV Breakout (Climactic Action Bar)

Magic 88004 · 0.02 lots · M5 · Teacher Lesson 2 / VSA Scenario 3 · BUY+SELL
Catch the reversal when a climax of selling exhausts, gets stop-hunted, then breaks back up. "Smart money shakes out weak hands, then runs."
BUY steps:
  1. Uptrend on M5 (price now is > price 2 hours / 24 bars ago, by > $2).
  2. Mark the retracement — the red candles in the last ~15 M5 bars.
  3. Find the Climactic Action Bar = the highest-volume RED candle in that retracement.
  4. Big-spread filter: that climax bar must be a large candle — its range ≥ 1.3× the average of the prior 10 bars. (Not just high volume — must be big.)
  5. Liquidity sweep: price must dip below the climax bar's low (the stop-hunt). This is Scenario 3.
  6. Breakout: a green candle closes above the climax bar's high (opened at/below it = a fresh break of the "upper trigger line").
  7. H1 FVG: an unfilled bullish H1 Fair Value Gap was tapped during the retracement.
Entry: market BUY at breakout close SL: climax bar low − $2.00 TP: +$7.50
SELL = the mirror: downtrend, highest-volume GREEN climax, big spread, sweep ABOVE its high, red candle breaks back below its low, bearish H1 FVG tapped.

S3 — Liquidity-Sweep Reversal

Magic 88003 · 0.03 lots · M5 · Teacher Lesson 10 · BUY only (gold is bullish)
The cleanest reversal entry: a green candle stabs below a recent low to grab liquidity, then snaps back inside on heavier volume = buyers absorbed the supply.
BUY steps:
  1. Uptrend on M5 (price now > price 24 bars ago, by > $1).
  2. Find a green candle whose low later got broken by red candles → those reds are the retracement.
  3. The signal is a green "wicking" candle that:
      a. wicks below one of those reds' lows (sweeps liquidity),
      b. closes back inside that red's range,
      c. has higher volume than that red.
  4. No-rejection filter: the green's upper wick ≤ 35% of its range (a big upper wick = rejection = skip).
  5. M5 FVG: an unfilled bullish M5 Fair Value Gap was tapped during the retracement.
Entry: market BUY at the wicking green's close SL: wicking green low − $2.00 TP: peak of last 10 M5 bars

NSND — No Supply / No Demand break

Magic 88006 · 0.01 lots · M1 · Teacher Lessons 6-7 · BUY+SELL
When volume dries up at a level, the pros aren't selling/buying there. The first break of that dead-volume candle shows their hand.
No-Supply (BUY) steps:
  1. Uptrend on M1 (price now > price 60 bars ago, by > $2).
  2. Prior climax: a big-volume sell candle came just before (institutions absorbing).
  3. No-Supply candle: a small-spread RED candle with dead volume — smaller than the previous bars (ideally < half).
  4. M15 FVG: an unfilled bullish M15 Fair Value Gap overlaps that candle's zone.
  5. Sweep + break: price sweeps the candle's low (with a wick), then breaks its high (with a body) → trigger.
Entry: market BUY at the break of the high SL: just below the candle (tiny) TP: +$12
No-Demand = the mirror in a downtrend: small-spread GREEN dead-volume candle, prior big buy climax, bearish M15 FVG, sweep its HIGH then break its LOW → SELL.
The golden rule across all three: each setup waits for a trend → retracement → volume signal → break. If the market is flat or choppy (e.g. the dead Asian hours), nothing fires — that's by design, not a bug. And each setup's Fair Value Gap comes from its own structure timeframe: S3→M5, NSND→M15, S1→H1. We never force trades; standing aside in chop is how the account survives.
