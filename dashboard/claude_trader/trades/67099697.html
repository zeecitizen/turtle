Why it didn't make money

1. A buy against the 15-minute trend, in chop

At the setup the 9 EMA (4157.94) was $11.93 under the 50 EMA (4169.87): the 15-minute trend was down, and gold had fallen $5.63 over the previous four hours. The efficiency ratio was 0.138 — the market was moving sideways. The pin bar was a bounce inside a falling market, and the bounce stopped at +$3.94.

2. The target was far for the move available

TP1 was the 50 EMA, $7.36 away, about 1.2 × the 15-minute ATR ($6.04). The best the bounce managed was $3.94. The 75% breakeven step needed $5.52, so the trade stayed at full risk the whole time.

3. The daily-close rule decided the exit

The trade was −$2.12 four minutes before the daily break, so the EA closed it (it shuts losing trades before the break, keeps winners). The stop at 4154.20 was never touched: after the break gold dipped to 4154.76 (00:00 server) — 56 cents above this stop — and then rallied to 4168.69 by 03:00. Holding through the break would have meant sitting through that dip at full 2-lot risk; the rule chose a known −$424.

The size: 2 lots on a $10,000 account

Over the full year (MT5, 1 lot) this EA had 27 losing streaks of 4+ trades (longest: 11), 29 of 184 trading days as bad as yesterday, a worst day of −$3,782 and a worst drawdown of $12,681. At 2 lots those become a worst day of −$7,563 and a drawdown of $25,361 — 254% of a $10,000 account. Every stop here cost $1,500–2,200 (15–22% of the account). The strategy is profitable over the year, but at this size its ordinary bad stretches empty the account first.

One-off or a pattern? The full-year check

Same EA, same rules, full year (Jan 6 – Oct 8 2026, MT5, Axi real ticks, 1 lot):

  • BUYs against the 15-minute trend (9 EMA under the 50 at the setup): 257 trades, 51% won, +$44,361. Profitable — this is where the strategy catches turns.
  • Setups with chop under 0.15: 166 trades, 52% won, +$52,087.
  • Trades shut by the daily-close rule: 30 trades, total −$7,988.

So this was a normal losing trade of a profitable kind of setup, not a broken rule. Report.md §10 measured the obvious fix — buy only with the trend — and it cut the year's profit by about three quarters.

What would have prevented it

No rule change would have saved this trade without costing more elsewhere (trend filters: report.md §10). What would have shrunk it: the size — at the designed 1 lot per $25,000 (0.4 lots on this account) this loss would have been about −$85.
