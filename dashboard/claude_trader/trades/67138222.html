Why it didn't make money

1. The setup type that loses money over the year

A BUY on the 50 EMA (Saad's "inverse" long). Over the full year this setup made 74 trades, won 43%, and lost −$892 in total, while the 9-EMA longs made money. This trade is the textbook case.

2. Bought the top of a rally, against the trend

Gold had just rallied $11.48 over four hours (from 4154.76 to the high 4168.69), but the 15-minute 9 EMA (4161.46) was still under the 50 (4162.78). The entry at 4169.02 was above the candle's high, right at the top of the move, in the most volatile hour of the night (ATR $8.79).

3. No target, the widest stop

With the exit EMA below the entry, the trade had no TP1 and no 75% breakeven step — only a trail that starts on the second candle. And because the candle was big, the stop was $10.90 away: $2,180 at 2 lots.

The size: 2 lots on a $10,000 account

Over the full year (MT5, 1 lot) this EA had 27 losing streaks of 4+ trades (longest: 11), 29 of 184 trading days as bad as yesterday, a worst day of −$3,782 and a worst drawdown of $12,681. At 2 lots those become a worst day of −$7,563 and a drawdown of $25,361 — 254% of a $10,000 account. Every stop here cost $1,500–2,200 (15–22% of the account). The strategy is profitable over the year, but at this size its ordinary bad stretches empty the account first.

One-off or a pattern? The full-year check

  • BUYs on the 50 EMA, full year: 74 trades, 43% won, −$892.
  • BUYs on the 9 EMA, full year: 399 trades, 46% won, +$103,137.

What would have prevented it

Tested and rejected: switching off the BUYs on the 50 EMA (InpLongRule = 0, a setting that already exists).

No 50-EMA buys (MT5, full year, Axi real ticks, 1 lot): net +$179,104 vs +$202,222 today's rules; Jan–Jun +$120,141 vs +$134,541; Jul–Oct +$58,963 vs +$67,681; max drawdown 13 223.40 (9.03%) vs 13 422.40 (8.35%).

Worse in both halves. On their own those trades lose a little, but when they're blocked the EA takes other setups in their place, and those do worse. So the category stays. What would have shrunk this loss is the size: at 0.2 lots on this account it would have been about −$220.
