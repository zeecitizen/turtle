Why this PC's EA tried those stop moves, and why none happened

The EA on this PC is an old version (v1.11). The position carries its magic number (88302), so it "adopted" the trade and treated it as a runner, trailing the stop 2 × ATR above the lowest price since entry. That's why the levels kept falling as gold fell. Algo Trading is off in this terminal, so every attempt was rejected and the real stop was never touched from here.

What the real stop probably did

That's up to the EA on the other machine. If it runs v1.17 or v1.18, it behaves like Axi's identical trade (#3, SELL 1 lot at 4165.48): the stop moved to the entry price once gold covered 75% of the way to TP1 (Axi did this at 21:53 PKT). After that the trade can't lose, apart from spread and commission.
