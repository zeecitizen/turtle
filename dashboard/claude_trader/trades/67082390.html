Why it didn't make money

1. The wrong chart and the wrong version

The 15-minute strategy ran on 1-minute candles, where its rules have no edge (report.md §5, §35: on M1 spread and noise eat everything). And it was v1.11 — six versions old, with the 30-pip stop and the broken long rule that v1.12 fixed.

2. Two copies on one account

This copy shared magic number 88302 with the real EA on the trading machine. Earlier the same day it also tried to move the stop of trade 67046250 (blocked while Algo Trading was off). Two copies with one magic number can adopt and manage each other's trades.

One-off or a pattern? The full-year check

Not applicable — this wasn't the strategy's decision.

What would have prevented it

One EA per account, with its own magic number. The v1.11 copy is now gone from Claude's PC (its last line is the 22:25 removal; nothing since). Keep the 15-minute EA on M15 charts only.
