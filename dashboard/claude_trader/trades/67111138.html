Why it didn't make money

1. A hole in the EA's gap protection (a real bug)

InpGapCancel is meant to drop a waiting setup when the market closes before it can trigger. In the code, at each new candle the EA first cancels a waiting setup if there was a gap, and only then looks for a new setup on the candle that just closed. At the reopen, the candle that just closed is the last one before the break — so a setup from it is created after the gap check, and its break is traded on the reopen candle, across the closure. The same thing can happen every daily reopen and every Monday open.

2. Bought into a fading, choppy market

The 9 EMA (4160.91) was $6.61 under the 50 (4167.52): the 15-minute trend was down. The reopen hour is thin (tick volume 1,381 vs 1,764–4,442 in the candles before the break). The trade never got more than 51 cents in profit.

3. The stop sat where stops get hunted

The stop (4154.77) was just under the night's cluster of lows (4155.06, 4155.48). Price took exactly that level and then reversed $8.83 within 15 minutes — a classic sweep of the stops under the lows. The EA's 35-pip stop is mechanical (candle low − $3.50), so it can't avoid such levels.

The size: 2 lots on a $10,000 account

Over the full year (MT5, 1 lot) this EA had 27 losing streaks of 4+ trades (longest: 11), 29 of 184 trading days as bad as yesterday, a worst day of −$3,782 and a worst drawdown of $12,681. At 2 lots those become a worst day of −$7,563 and a drawdown of $25,361 — 254% of a $10,000 account. Every stop here cost $1,500–2,200 (15–22% of the account). The strategy is profitable over the year, but at this size its ordinary bad stretches empty the account first.

One-off or a pattern? The full-year check

On Axi's full year this gap case happened only 2 times (+$869 in total) — Axi's break falls at a different hour than PrimeXBT's. So it's rare, but it's a genuine hole: the EA does something its own setting says it shouldn't.

What would have prevented it

Fix the gap hole: don't create a setup from the last candle before a market closure. Built and tested as Saad_gapfix.mq5.

Gap fix (MT5, full year, Axi real ticks, 1 lot): net +$201,361 vs +$202,222 today's rules; Jan–Jun +$133,680 vs +$134,541; Jul–Oct +$67,681 vs +$67,681; max drawdown 14 115.00 (23.97%) vs 13 422.40 (8.35%).

On Axi's year it removes just two trades, and one of them was a big winner, so it costs $861 (Jul–Oct unchanged). It makes the EA do what its own setting promises, but it is not a profit fix. Your call whether to ship it; I'd keep it as an option.
