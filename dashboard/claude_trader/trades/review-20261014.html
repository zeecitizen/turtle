The causes, biggest first

1. The size: 2 lots on $10,000 is five times the designed risk

The EA was built and tested at 1 lot per $25,000. Over the full year (MT5, Axi real ticks, 1 lot) it made +$202,222. Along the way it had 27 losing streaks of 4+ trades (the longest was 11), 29 days out of 184 as bad as yesterday, a worst day of −$3,782 and a worst drawdown of $12,681.

At 2 lots on $10,000 the same ordinary bad stretch becomes a worst day of −$7,563 and a drawdown of $25,361, which is 254% of the account. Yesterday wasn't unusual for the strategy. The size turned it into a 35% loss.

2. A normal losing streak: buys into a falling, choppy market

All four big losers were BUYs while the 15-minute 9 EMA was under the 50 EMA. The chop reading was near the EA's 0.11 limit, and most of them went almost nothing our way (+$0.37 to +$3.94) before reversing. Both winners were SELLs with the trend.

But over the full year these are not losing categories:

  • buys against the 15-minute trend: +$44,361 (257 trades)
  • low-chop setups: +$53,561
  • "no target" buys: +$77,462

The strategy makes its money at turns, so it has days like this. Report.md §10 tested "buy only with the trend": it cut the year's profit by about 75%.

3. Operations: two bots on one account, a wrong chart, a shaky connection

  • Two copies of the EA share account 1364880 and magic number 88302: v1.17 on the trading machine and an old v1.11 on Claude's PC. On the 13th the v1.11 copy tried to move 67046250's stop. After its chart was switched to M1, it opened 67082390 off a 1-minute candle. It has since been removed (nothing logged after 10:25 PM).
  • The trading machine kept reconnecting. On the 14th from 7:10 to 8:26 AM PKT the account was re-authorised from 119.154.92.250 every few minutes, after the connection quality dropped to 46%. Stops live at the broker, so no trade lost money because of it. But the breakeven, TP1 and trail steps need the EA online.
  • On Axi, VSISA is attached to an H1 chart (loaded 2:42 AM today). VSISA reads the chart's candles, so on H1 it trades hourly candles, not the 5-minute ones it was tested on.

4. A code hole: setups across the daily break

InpGapCancel cancels a waiting setup when the market closes. But a setup formed by the last candle before the break is only created at the reopen, after that check, and then traded on the reopen candle. That was trade 67111138 (−$1,524). It's fixed in a research copy (Saad_gapfix.mq5).

What to change

  1. Size: 0.2 lots on $10,000 (a worst-year drawdown of about 25% of the account). Trading 2 lots needs about $50,000.
  2. One EA per account. Remove every other copy (old versions on other terminals, the VPS, phones). Give any second EA its own InpMagicNumber.
  3. The 15-minute EA on M15 charts only, and VSISA on M5. TradeLifecycle v1.03 now draws the two EMAs, so a wrong timeframe is easy to spot.
  4. A stable connection for the trading machine (or a VPS), so the breakeven, TP1 and trail steps happen on time.
  5. Optional: ship the gap fix as a setting, for correctness.
