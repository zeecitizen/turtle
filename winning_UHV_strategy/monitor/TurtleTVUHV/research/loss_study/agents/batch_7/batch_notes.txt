BATCH 7 (21 losses 4 Aug-29 Sep, 42 twins). PYTHON HYPOTHESIS ONLY - NOT PROMOTED (CLAUDE.md). With a 0.1-pip target, fill and spread modelling decides almost every trade, so this setting is the one most exposed to the Python/MT5 gap; no net $ or 'WR after' below is evidence. Next step: MT5 Strategy Tester on the custom symbol.

1) WHAT THE CHARTS SHOW. I read all 63 charts, plus my own zoomed 1-min + 10-hour 5-min-context versions (agents/batch_7/zoom/). Every stopped loser has best_R <= 0.005: the breakout tick was the exact turning point and price never came back 0.1 pip. In most normal-hours losses a twin entered the same context (often the same price) minutes apart and won.

2) MAIN FINDING FOR NORMAL HOURS = NULL. Outside the rollover hour and the Friday last hour, a gradient-boosted model on ~100 causal features (trend, range position, swing structure, humps, volume, volatility, time) gives train->test AUC 0.563 and test->train 0.542, against a permutation-null 95th percentile of 0.580 (logistic regression: 0.548). Normal-hours losses do not separate from winners. Geometry explains why. TP = 0.1 pip beyond the fill, and buys fill at the ask over bid-based levels (0925: fill 1.14109 sat above the entry bar's bid high 1.14107). A random walk therefore loses with probability ~(spread + 0.1)/(stop + spread + 0.1) ~ 0.35/20.35 ~ 2%, which is the observed 2.26%. Only what widens the effective spread moves the loss rate: rollover, the weekend close, thin tape, spike ticks. Inside those windows it is 15-36%.

4) CAVEATS.
- The base loss rate differs by half: train 3.36% vs test 1.40%.
- Thresholds were chosen with both halves visible. The time rules sit on plateaus: Friday NY>=15:30..16:45 is x6.5-x23.8 everywhere; rollover windows 16:40-17:30 .. 16:45-17:45 are >= x5.3 train. Slip 1.2 sits near the train edge (1.0 = x1.99). vm30 <15/<20/<25 = x6.0/x5.5/x3.7 train.
- Time rules are coded in New York time (DST-aware). The data's weekend gap starts 20:58 UTC in summer and 21:58 UTC before DST, so an EA should key on the broker's 17:00 NY rollover.
- Variants scored but not listed: rollover NY 16:40-18:30 = 21L/199W (x3.76/x4.89); Friday NY>=16:30 = 10L/23W (x13.53/x16.25).
- EA note for slip: test (ask - level) for buys and (level - bid) for sells at the trigger tick.
