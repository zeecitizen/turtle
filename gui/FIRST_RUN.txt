TURTLE DESKTOP - first run
==========================

Turtle Desktop is the control panel for a vision-driven trading system: a mechanical
layer finds candidate setups, Claude LOOKS at the chart and decides take or skip, and an
MQL5 Expert Advisor executes and manages the exit.

WHAT YOU NEED ON THIS PC
  1. Python 3.12 or newer          https://www.python.org/downloads/
     (tick "Add Python to PATH" during setup)
  2. MetaTrader 5, logged into a DEMO account
  3. TradingView Desktop, logged in

FIRST START
  Open "Turtle Desktop" from the Start menu.
  Press  START EVERYTHING       - launches TradingView with its debug port, the data
                                  bridge and the dashboards.
  Set the TradingView chart to OANDA:XAUUSD (or COINBASE:BTCUSD at the weekend).
  In MT5, attach the EA from the mt5\ folder to that chart, press F7 to compile,
  and switch Algo Trading ON.  Keep it on a DEMO account.
  Press  LAUNCH CLAUDE SESSION  - opens Claude Code on this folder, pre-prompted to read
                                  CLAUDE_REALTIME_EA.md and resume live judging.

WHAT THE PANEL SHOWS
  LIVE (from MT5)   real bid/ask, spread, balance, equity and any running position P&L
  ARMED             a retracement and its UHV exist; the breakout has not fired yet
  TODAY'S TRADES    every judged setup - double-click a row for the full story:
                    the chart, why Claude took or skipped it, your own comment box,
                    and the two grading buttons (10/10 or 0/10)
  PDF               "PDF: failed setups" writes one page per losing trade for study

IF THE POWER GOES OUT
  Press POWER OUTAGE. It restarts TradingView with its debug port, the tunnel, the data
  bridge and the dashboards, and clears any stale setup so nothing old is ever traded.

THE PLAYBOOK
  CLAUDE_REALTIME_EA.md is the whole system in writing - rules, architecture, recovery
  steps and the mistakes already paid for. Read it before changing anything.

DEMO ONLY until it has proven itself on your own account.
